Your fit, measured with care.

Camera assisted measurement prototype. Annotate calibrated front and side photos. Results are estimates, not validated tailoring measurements.

Capture setup and measurement definitions
  1. Have a helper use the rear camera. Wear close fitting clothing, stand barefoot and keep your posture neutral. Keep arms slightly away from the torso for the front view and out of the outline for the side view.
  2. Place a rigid vertical reference of measured length beside you, at the same distance from the camera as your body. Do this separately in both views. A 100 cm rod is useful. Do not use a reference on the wall behind you.
  3. Use the normal lens, avoid ultrawide and zoom, keep the camera level around mid body height and stand several metres away. Include the reference, shoulders and floor without cropping.
  4. Mark shoulder tip for width; shoulder neck point for vertical lengths; fullest bust and hip levels; natural waist. Mark each cross section's two outline edges at the same height. Match anatomical levels across views.
  5. Skirt length starts at the side waist and ends at your chosen hem. Trouser length is the outseam to the chosen hem, not the inseam. Shoulder lengths are projected straight lines, not a tape following the body's surface.
Loose fabric, body rotation, camera perspective and non elliptical body shape can materially change results. Verify with a tape before pattern cutting.
